# Wishlist

Wishlist gives you an allowance that grows by the second. Paste a product link and the name, photo and price fill in; prices are checked daily and you are told when they drop.

Each item has to cool off before it is ready to buy, by a rule you set such as 3 days for every $100. Buying before then freezes your balance for 7 days. Friends can gift an item from a list you share.

Open the app after installing and sign up: the first account is yours.

Source: https://github.com/armanckeser/wishlist
